# v0.2.x — Phase 1: host engine core

First end-to-end light on real LEDs (`research/synthesis/architecture.md` §11
Phase 1). A pure render core with the ADR-005 authoring contract, the §8 dev
loop (preview sink → hot reload → golden frames), one paced clock (ADR-010),
and DDP 8-bit out to a stock WLED node (ADR-003/004). Everything later builds
on this; nothing here is throwaway.

**Series is gated.** Phase 1 opens when the v0.1.x Phase-0 gates clear. As of
2026-07-27 all three have desktop verdicts — item 1 PASS at 52× (2026-07-15),
item 2 GO (2026-07-16), item 13 PASS (2026-07-27) — each pending a rerun on
whatever becomes the deployed host. Only the DDP-sink story is still hard
blocked, on hardware.

Two obligations carry in from Phase 0 and are stories here, not afterthoughts:
the §12 item-2 verdict was **GO conditional** on the idiom-kit additions, and
the sdf3d dead-`spin` addendum is why param-liveness is a harness check rather
than a code-review habit.

**Progress (2026-08-20).** The Authoring-surface epic is complete and the
§12 item-2 GO is no longer conditional: the render core, the four mandated
idiom-kit helpers, the authoring docs, and the stumble-#17 decision all
landed, with all five study patterns ported bit-identically and golden-frame
tests running against the real engine. The contract is frozen as amended.
The paced clock (ADR-010) landed 2026-08-20 with the newest-wins
`SinkDriver` backpressure seam, and the sink story landed the same day:
the `Sink` protocol, ANSI + browser-preview sinks, the
`python -m pewpew run` entry point, and the playground retirement
(ADR-020 added `websockets` as the second runtime dep). What remains in
this series: hot reload, DDP (hardware-blocked), and the param-liveness
half of the typed-params story.

#### Authoring surface

Shared context: ADR-005 (Accepted 2026-07-26) is the contract —
`before_render(dt, features)` + `render(x, y, z, index)` over normalized 0–1
coordinate **arrays**, persistent `self.pixels`, time-parameterized motion,
per-frame exception containment. `bench/authoring/surface.py` is the
throwaway prototype of it; the five patterns in `bench/authoring/` are the
acceptance corpus — they must port to the real engine with their behavior
unchanged. Stumble numbers refer to `bench/results/authoring-2026-07-16.md`.

- [x] **Pure render core + pattern contract**
    - Effort: L
    - What: `render(t, mapping, params, seed) → frame` as a pure function, with the ADR-005 pattern base class, normalized world coordinates, persistent frame buffer, and per-frame exception containment.
    - Why: Every other story in this series and every later phase sits on it; purity is what makes golden frames, deterministic scrubbing, and the fleet twin possible (§8, T-1).
    - Dependencies: v0.1.x §12 items 1–2
    - Status: Completed (2026-07-27)
    - Notes: `src/pewpew/{pattern,kit,render,mapping,features}.py`; supersedes `bench/authoring/surface.py`. All five patterns ported to `src/pewpew/patterns/` and proven **bit-identical** (`np.array_equal`, not RMS-tolerant) against the prototype over the study's own 120 frames at seed 42 — `tests/test_port_equivalence.py`. Purity lands at the *sequence* level, not the frame level: ADR-005 patterns are deliberately stateful, so `render_sequence(factory, mapping, params, seed, frames, fps)` is the pure function §8 means, and `Renderer.advance_to(t)`/`.step(dt)` is the live form the paced clock will drive. Timestamps accumulate `t += 1.0/fps` to match `FakeClock` bit-for-bit without importing it (engine must not depend on the harness). `src/harness/` untouched. Also landed: the `time()` module-global hack is gone (frame-scoped `ContextVar`), and a `params` key naming an undeclared `Param` now raises rather than creating a dead knob.

- [x] **Idiom-kit additions mandated by the §12 item-2 verdict**
    - Effort: M
    - What: Add `fade(dt, tau)`, `bins(x, n)`, `sample2d(field, x, y)`, and `hash01(x, salt)` to the standard idiom kit alongside the ADR-005 built-ins (`time()`, `wave()`, `triangle()`, `square()`).
    - Why: The authoring-surface GO was *conditional* on these four — they shield the stumbles the study measured (threshold/dtype edges, frame-rate-dependent decay, per-pixel hashing).
    - Dependencies: Pure render core
    - Status: Completed (2026-07-27)
    - Notes: In `src/pewpew/kit.py`, each a bit-exact substitute for the study expression it replaces, with `tests/test_kit.py` asserting that against the inlined original. Shipped as `hash01(x, seed, salt)` — **seed and salt stay two arguments, deliberately**: the study's `12.9898*x64 + self.seed + salt` associates left-to-right, and a pre-summed salt diverges by 1 ulp on 46 of the study's 120 frames. That is stumble #6's own failure mode one step upstream, so the signature reproduces the study by construction rather than by luck. `fade` is dt-based per Q-5. Adopted at every call site except fire's `lift`, which is `exp(-dt*rise)`, not `exp(-dt/tau)`.

- [x] **Authoring docs for the three measured friction points**
    - Effort: S
    - What: Document the internal-field idiom, that `np.where` evaluates both branches (so guards must be safe, not lazy), and where RNG belongs so patterns stay seed-reproducible.
    - Why: 13 of the study's 17 stumbles were expertise-shielded — an author without NumPy fluency hits them. Docs are the cheapest mitigation the study identified.
    - Dependencies: Idiom-kit additions
    - Status: Completed (2026-07-27)
    - Notes: `docs/authoring.md`. Covers the mandated three (#12/#15, #5, #14) plus the three the study's own "Reading of the evidence" clusters with them: private per-pixel state (#17), dt-decay (#2), and threshold/dtype edges (#6, #9). Every claim cites its `# STUMBLE-N` anchor in `bench/authoring/*.py`, and every example was executed against the real engine rather than written from the docstrings.

- [x] **Decide and implement private per-pixel state buffers (stumble #17)**
    - Effort: M
    - What: Decide whether patterns may declare private per-pixel arrays alongside the sanctioned `self.pixels`, and implement the outcome (including lifecycle across hot reload).
    - Why: The study left this open; fire and any particle pattern need per-pixel state beyond the output buffer, so leaving it undecided blocks a faithful port.
    - Dependencies: Pure render core
    - Status: Completed (2026-07-27)
    - Notes: **Decided yes**, recorded as `## Amendment (2026-07-27)` in ADR-005 with architecture §3 following it. A `setup(n, x, y, z, index)` hook hands the pattern its coordinates once per binding; `self.buffer(name, shape)` declared there is engine-managed (zeroed on rebind, reallocated on mapping change, read-only inside `render`). **Coords-at-setup is the actual unlock** — #17's bind was that `before_render` never sees coordinates. Precedent converged across three lineages: ISF's manifest-declared host-allocated `PERSISTENT` buffers [S218], Shadertoy's previous-state/next-state buffers [S219], and Pixelblaze's persistent arrays [S680] — the last of which pays for them with an *impure render*, the trade we explicitly reject. Registry keys on name+spec, never array identity, because authors rebind (fire does). Two follow-ons this leaves open, both for the hot-reload story: `setup()` failures are deliberately *not* contained (they propagate out of `Renderer.__init__`, so the reload supervisor must keep the last-good binding itself), and scalar per-pattern state (`self.phase`, `self.head`) is not engine-tracked, so reload must decide whether it carries. Also worth a later pass: comet's sparkle still uses the study's time-salted workaround rather than a private buffer, since "behavior unchanged" was the port's acceptance bar — so the corpus does not yet exercise the feature #17 motivated (`docs/authoring.md` §2 demonstrates it instead). Unblocks "Port the upgraded fire to Python" in `unscheduled.md`.

- [ ] **Typed params (§5) + param-liveness check in the L1 harness**
    - Effort: M
    - What: Typed `Param` declarations per §5/ADR-006, plus a harness check that wiggles each declared param across its range and asserts the frame sequence actually changes.
    - Why: The sdf3d `spin` parameter was a mathematical no-op and *nothing* caught it — not the authoring surface, not golden frames, because other motion masked it.
    - Dependencies: Pure render core
    - Status: Not Started
    - Notes: Addendum in `bench/results/authoring-2026-07-16.md` is the motivating evidence. The check belongs in the L1 pyramid level next to `tests/test_golden_frames.py` so it runs in `uv run pytest`. Carried in from review: a `params` override naming an undeclared `Param` is now refused, but declared `lo`/`hi` are **not** range-checked, so `{"speed": 1e9}` is accepted. That inconsistency is deliberate for now — this story owns param semantics, and modulation endpoints (§5) may legitimately drive a knob past its UI range — but decide it here rather than leaving it as an accident.

#### Frame pipeline & sinks

Shared context: §8 gives the leverage order — render core + preview sink
first, hot reload second, browser preview third, golden frames from day one.
The preview fixes already landed carry one hard-won requirement forward: any
preview sink must apply display gamma, or low-end glow crushes to black.

- [x] **Paced render clock, deadline-corrected (ADR-010)**
    - Effort: M
    - What: One paced clock measuring elapsed, sleeping the remainder, and correcting accumulated error; per-sink newest-wins backpressure bounded at ~2 frames in flight.
    - Why: ADR-010's core mechanism; the single clock is also where modulated params and audio features resolve exactly once per frame.
    - Dependencies: Pure render core
    - Status: Completed (2026-08-20)
    - Notes: `src/pewpew/pace.py` — `PacedLoop` is the bench `deadline` pacer ported exactly (absolute schedule seeded once, skip-ahead with the no-`+1` rule and its rationale comment carried over), plus the spin variant as a `spin_margin` option with `measure_spin_margin()` for the host probe; the engine never auto-probes. The open timestamp question got decided and recorded as `## Amendment (2026-08-20)` in ADR-010: **engine time is nominal schedule time, never measured wall time** — `t += 1/fps` per tick by repeated addition, one further period per skipped slot — so a live show is bit-identical to `render_sequence` at the same slot (`tests/test_pace.py::test_engine_time_matches_render_sequence_bitwise`, `np.array_equal`). Working the skip arithmetic through fixed two wrong intuitions worth keeping: a 2.5-period stall drops exactly *one* slot (the next frame renders immediately, late in the last past slot, rather than idling), and a sub-2-period stall drops *zero* — both are what the no-`+1` rule buys. Backpressure ships as `SinkDriver` (per-sink worker + 1-slot mailbox = Fadecandy's 2-in-flight bound); `PacedLoop` itself takes plain `on_frame(t, frame)` callables handed one shared read-only copy per tick, so the Sink protocol stays owned by the sink story, which wraps its `send` in a `SinkDriver`. Seams left deliberately: `call_soon(fn)` is the loop-thread mutation point hot reload will use, and a callback exception (including an uncaught bind failure) propagates out of `run()` — the reload supervisor owns catching it. Features already resolve once per frame inside `advance_to`; the param tree will hook the same place (typed-params story). GC posture: one frame copy + one tuple per tick, no growing containers — the ADR-010 GC retest obligation (re-bench once sinks + param tree + hot reload exist) stands unchanged.

- [x] **Preview sink + ANSI sink**
    - Effort: M
    - What: Sink interface with browser preview over websocket (map positions once, then compact color buffers) and an ANSI terminal sink for ssh/CI.
    - Why: The no-hardware dev path — §8 makes preview-as-a-sink the first thing after the render core, and it costs ~nothing beside DDP.
    - Dependencies: Pure render core
    - Status: Completed (2026-08-20)
    - Notes: `src/pewpew/sinks/` — `base.py` owns the `Sink` protocol (`send(t, frame)` + `start`/`stop`; ADR-003's fill/latch requirement is honored by *fusing* both into `send`, recorded in the module docstring), the shared `to_display_u8` gamma+quantize helper (the Q-1 quantize-once point; the `b68b96a` display gamma, deliberately distinct from ADR-001's Phase-2 LED curve), and `RateGate` — sinks self-pace by blocking their `SinkDriver` worker, so the driver's newest-wins mailbox *is* the frame-dropper (ADR-010's "each sink paces itself"). `ansi.py`: layout computed once from the mapping (1D row / half-block 2D, y=0 bottom / 3D z-slices left→right per `lattice()`'s design); cells average in linear light *before* gamma; frame→string is pure, so `tests/goldens/ansi_grid4x4.txt` is an exact-string golden. `preview.py`: `websockets.sync.server` (**new runtime dep, ADR-020** — sync/threading server matches the threaded engine; one port serves page + upgrade), decimation to `max_pixels=4096` at hello time [S508], per-client `SinkDriver` fan-out (a dead tab is contained, proven in `tests/test_preview_sink.py`), payload carries current-status-not-delta so coalescing can't lose an error transition. `SinkDriver` in `pace.py` went generic over payload (hint-only). Runner: `python -m pewpew run <pattern> [--map] [--ansi] [--preview]` — minimal, hot reload extends it. Both sinks take a `status` supplier; the runner wires `renderer.error` into it (red line / banner beside the pulsing-red frame) — the same seam hot reload's supervisor will use. Verified live: 120 frames @ 60 fps with both sinks, 0 skips; break-and-heal surfaces and clears in both. three.js `Points` for 3D deferred to Phase 3 (z-slice 2D layout instead, matching ANSI). **Landing sinks is one of ADR-010's three GC-retest triggers** (with param tree + hot reload) — the soak obligation stands. Review pass 2026-08-21 closed seven findings, four of them on the runner rather than the sinks: the `status` supplier read `renderer.error` twice (a pattern healing between the two reads painted the string `None` in red), the CLI configured no log handler at all — so the preview URL was invisible *and* `Renderer._contain`'s traceback went to stderr straight through the ANSI rewind block, desyncing it permanently — the status line's row was not reserved in the height budget, and `SinkDriver.stop()`'s "transport still in use" bool was discarded by its only caller. The runner now owns log presentation (`_DeferredLog` holds records while `AnsiSink.interactive` is true, replaying on exit; the sink's red status line stays the in-band channel), sniffs the terminal size itself (`AnsiLayout` keeps its 80x24 defaults so `render_frame` stays pure and the golden stays valid), and takes a `now`/`sleep` seam like every other paced component. `SinkDriver` gained `drain()` — `stop()` discards the pending frame by design, so `--frames N` was reliably losing frame N. Left deliberately unchanged: `_axis_res` sizes by distinct-value count while `bins()` positions over [0, 1], so a mapping spanning only part of the unit range under-fills the grid. The browser preview's `makeLayout` makes the same assumption, and that shared assumption is what makes the two previews agree — normalizing one alone would split them. Documented on `from_mapping`; the fix for both is a story in `unscheduled.md` under imported maps.

- [ ] **Hot reload with state carry-over**
    - Effort: M
    - What: watchfiles-driven re-import of the edited pattern module while the engine runs; `t`, parameter values, and the frame buffer carry across the swap.
    - Why: The dev loop is a first-class deliverable — Pixelblaze's editor is the UX benchmark and a Python engine gets that loop with no compile step at all.
    - Dependencies: Pure render core; private-state-buffer decision (buffers must carry too)
    - Status: Not Started
    - Notes: Leaf-module re-import, not jurigged-style whole-process hot-patching (§8). A failed reload must keep the last-good pattern running with a visible error state, never stop the show (ADR-005). **Unblocked 2026-07-27** — the buffer registry exists and keys on name+spec, which is the unit buffers carry across by. Three things this story must settle, all left open deliberately by the core: (1) `setup()` failures are *not* contained — they propagate out of `Renderer.__init__`/`rebind`, so this supervisor must catch bind failures and keep the previous binding; (2) scalar per-pattern state (`self.phase`, `self.head`) is not in the registry, so decide whether it carries or re-initializes — today buffers would carry and scalars would reset, which is a silent inconsistency; (3) per-frame containment already self-heals, since the renderer keeps calling a throwing pattern every frame and clears `renderer.error` on the first success.

- [ ] **DDP 8-bit sink to a stock WLED node**
    - Effort: M
    - What: DDP output at 8 bits to stock WLED over WiFi, as the day-one carrier before any custom receiver firmware exists.
    - Why: First real light. ADR-004 makes stock WLED the adequate interim receiver so Phase 1 ships without firmware work.
    - Dependencies: Pure render core; ESP32 receiver + strip on hand
    - Status: Blocked
    - Notes: Quick-start cart (~$114) in `docs/hardware-buying-list.md`. SK6812 is RGBW (4 bytes/px) — the DDP output must send the W channel and WLED needs RGBW mode. §12 item 14 (sustained per-receiver ingest) is the natural follow-on bench once hardware lands.
