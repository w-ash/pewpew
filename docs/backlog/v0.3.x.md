# v0.3.x — Layered effects, effect programs and agent connectivity

pewpew renders one pattern at a time, and its idiom kit has no noise,
palettes, folds or 3D sampling. This series makes it a layered effects
engine for 1D, 2D and 3D maps: a stack of layers (ADR-021), each running a
typed graph of ops (ADR-022), modulated by signals, and an agent surface
(ADR-023) through which an agent can read the catalog, check a look, see
and measure what it renders, and drive a running engine within the T-7
tiers.

**Goals** (the maintainer's, as paraphrased in the series plan): a deep,
Winamp-visualizer-grade effects tool for 1D, 2D and 3D layouts, that people
find easy to use, that AI agents can connect to deeply, and whose code is
elegant, DRY, composable 2026 Python. Judge new ops and phases against them.
The research behind the series, with RA-25's measurements and the options
not taken, is the artifact "pewpew Effects Options" (2026-11-04, not
public); `unscheduled.md` lists the options not built.

**Branching.** Every story is a branch and worktree off the integration
branch `fx`, merged into `fx` after review. Looks version 4 breaks the
deployed looks file, and launchd runs the aura from the repo checkout
(`deploy/launchd/pewpew-aura.plist`), so `fx` merged into `main` only at
the maintainer's call (e37aa7d).

**Gates: cleared.** Typed params (v0.2.8) are on `main` (merge 6a2ea0f), and
`fx` was cut from it.

**Split stories.** Four stories were built ahead of the layer stack, on
files no other story touched: the effect primitives (from "Effect
programs"), warpable patterns (from "Effect programs"), contact sheets,
metrics and PNG (from "Agent core"), and observe (from "Live look
control").

#### Effects engine

Shared context. ADR-021 (the stack), ADR-022 (programs, the catalog, the
codec), and the 2026-11-05 amendments to ADR-005 (warped coordinates, a
stable `index`, `Coords.u`, `warpable`) and ADR-006 (the `palette` kind,
layered paths, routes, looks v4). Seams to reuse, not copy:
`pewpew.params` (the one param write path, which keeps the base value, and
`Bound` for modulation), `FrameSource` and `Live` in
`src/pewpew/render.py`, the before and after stages of `PacedLoop` and
`Calls.ask` (`src/pewpew/pace.py`), `AuraControl.request` and `Deck`
(`src/pewpew/aura/`), `errors.scrub`, and the wire golden
(`tests/goldens/wire.json`) with `web/src/infra/wire/protocol.ts`. Rules
for every story: no wall clock, no unseeded RNG and no `hash()` for
seeds; float32 arrays; existing goldens unchanged; the L1 suite under 30 s.
